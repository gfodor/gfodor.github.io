The contrast between vignettes 01 and 02 is the design test. The first is concentrated: one line of code, in one file, doing all the lifting. The second is the opposite shape: four files cooperating around the absence of a function. If the format works for both, it works.

The brief was deliberate that algorithmic vignettes would be the easier case. Vignette 02 is the empirical answer to whether the form survives weaker algorithmic spines.
